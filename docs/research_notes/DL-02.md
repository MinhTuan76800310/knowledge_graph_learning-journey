## What this source establishes for Ch4
The classic optimized-tableau ALC/SHIQ reasoner that established the tableau decision-procedure tradition HermiT later superseded. Anchors the Ch4 point that class-expression satisfiability and property reasoning are computed by tableau-style procedures.

## Safe simplifications
Describing it as a tableau-based DL reasoner is safe.

## Dangerous simplifications / limits
Do not claim it implements OWL 2 (it targets SHIQ/ALC-era DLs); it is a lineage anchor, not a current OWL 2 tool.
