The canonical DL reference behind OWL's design: the interpretation -> model -> entailment mechanism, TBox/ABox/RBox organization, classes-as-sets semantics (subclass/equivalence/disjointness), and the syntax-vs-model-theoretic-semantics distinction. Cited in the Ch4 'In practice' callout as the theoretical anchor for the whole formal-meaning apparatus.

## Safe simplifications
Naming it as the standard reference for DL formal semantics is safe.

## Dangerous simplifications / limits
Do not cite it as an OWL-specific document; it predates OWL 2 and describes DL families generally, not the OWL 2 profiles.
