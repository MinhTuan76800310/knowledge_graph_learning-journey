## Key Points

- Defines concept drift: change in data distribution over time; types (sudden, gradual, recurring).
- Surveys drift detection, adaptation, and learning in evolving environments.

## Semantic Contract

- The book uses the concept-drift framework to describe knowledge staleness and degradation over time.
- Drift in the data world corresponds to epistemic staleness in the KG world.
- MUST NOT: claim concept-drift methods directly apply to KGs without adaptation; the mapping is analogical and structural.
