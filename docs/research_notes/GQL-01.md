## Key Points

- GQL (Graph Query Language) is the ISO/IEC 39075:2024 international standard
  for querying property graphs.
- Published 2024-04-17.
- GQL "builds on the foundations of SQL" and "integrates proven ideas" from
  openCypher, PGQL, GSQL, and G-CORE.
- GQL is the standard for property graph querying, analogous in role (but not
  in mechanism) to SPARQL for RDF.

## Semantic Contract for Chapter 2

- GQL is the ISO standard for graph query languages.
- Cypher has significant GQL alignment but is NOT identical to GQL.
- Do not conflate Cypher with GQL in teaching.
- GQL is relevant context for the property graph side of Chapter 2.
