## Key Points

- Surveys temporal knowledge graph completion (TKGC): methods that incorporate timestamps into KGC to capture how facts change over time.
- Groups approaches by how they use timestamps (time-aware embeddings, temporal rules, etc.).
- Identifying that KGs are not static — the temporal dimension is essential for correctness.

## Semantic Contract

- The book uses this to support the temporal dimension of the living system: knowledge changes over time, and the system must operate on time-aware representations.
