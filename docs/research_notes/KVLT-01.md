# KVLT-01: Knowledge Vault — A Web-Scale Approach to Probabilistic Knowledge Fusion

- **Primary reference:** Dong, X., Gabrilovich, E., Heitz, G., Horn, W., Lao, N., Murphy, K., Strohmann, T., Sun, S., Zhang, W. (2014). "Knowledge Vault: A Web-Scale Approach to Probabilistic Knowledge Fusion." *KDD 2014*.
- **DOI:** 10.1145/2623330.2623623
- **URL:** https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2623330.2623623
- **Status:** FETCHED_AND_VERIFIED (DBLP metadata verified, 2026-08-31)
- **Used in:** Chapter 10

## Key Points

- Web-scale knowledge base construction combining extraction from web content with probabilistic reasoning from existing KB facts.
- Uses prior knowledge (existing KB) to predict new facts, then confirms via extraction.
- Introduces the Kalman-filter-style fusion of prior and extrinsic evidence.

## Semantic Contract

- The fusion of prior knowledge and extrinsic evidence is instructive for the living system's re-assessment mechanism.
- The book uses this as an operational example of maintenance-level knowledge evaluation.
- MUST NOT: claim KV is a living system in the book's sense; it is an acquisition/fusion system.
