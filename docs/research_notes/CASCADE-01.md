## Key Points

- Defines "data cascades": compounding events causing negative downstream effects from data issues.
- Cascades are triggered by low data quality, and are often invisible because data work is undervalued.
- Most cascades are discovered post-deployment, making them a maintenance and governance problem.

## Semantic Contract

- Data cascades = the cost of deferring data quality, analogous to the book's "knowledge debt" and "contradiction debt."
- The book uses this to reinforce that monitoring alone is insufficient — cascades are structural.
- MUST NOT: claim cascades are the same as feedback collapse; they are triggered by different root causes.
