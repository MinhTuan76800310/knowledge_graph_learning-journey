## Key Points (verified)

- The book's Chapter 6 is titled **"Creation and Enrichment"** and Chapter 7 is titled
  **"Quality Assessment"** (verified against the official table of contents).
- The Creation and Enrichment chapter treats the construction of knowledge graphs from
  data and text, including extraction and the resolution of entities across sources.
- The Quality Assessment chapter covers evaluating the quality of a knowledge graph.

## Semantic Contract

- The two Hogan chapters provide an academic framing for the Ch7 pipeline:
  creation/enrichment (acquisition + integration) and quality assessment (validation).
- Use as a secondary source that corroborates the pipeline's stage structure; the W3C

  models carry the formal weight.
