# KGQ-01: Quality Assessment for Linked Data — A Survey

- **Primary reference:** Zaveri, A., Rula, A., Maurino, A., Pietrobon, R., Lehmann, J., Auer, S. (2016). "Quality assessment for Linked Data: A survey." *Semantic Web* 7(1):63–93.
- **DOI:** 10.3233/SW-150175
- **URL:** https://www.semantic-web-journal.net/content/quality-assessment-linked-data-survey
- **Status:** FETCHED_AND_VERIFIED (SWJ page fetched, 2026-08-31)
- **Used in:** Chapter 10

## Key Points

- Comprehensive survey of Linked Data quality assessment frameworks and methodologies.
- Defines quality dimensions: accuracy, completeness, consistency, conciseness, timeliness, trustworthiness, availability, interlinking, performance, licensing, security, provenance.
- Each dimension has metrics and assessment methods.

## Semantic Contract

- Quality dimensions from this survey inform the book's five-dimension model (correctness, completeness, freshness, consistency, trustworthiness).
- The survey's "dimensions + metrics" framework is used as supporting evidence, not as a prescriptive standard.
- MUST NOT: claim the survey defines exactly the five dimensions the book uses; the book selects and adapts.
