## What this source establishes for Ch5
The standard reference for Datalog's fixpoint semantics and evaluation strategies (naive and
semi-naive). It confirms that the safe, function-free Horn rules the chapter uses for forward
chaining are exactly Datalog, and that the termination/monotonicity properties stated in §5.2 are
the textbook Datalog properties.

## Safe simplifications
Citing this survey as the canonical Datalog fixpoint-semantics reference is safe.

## Dangerous simplifications / limits
Authors verified via DBLP as Green, Huang, Loo, Zhou — do NOT attribute to any other pair
(an earlier draft note wrongly guessed "Cianflone & Mecca"; that is wrong and must not appear).
