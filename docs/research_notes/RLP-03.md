# RLP-03: Overview of the Rule Interchange Format (RIF)

- **URL:** https://www.w3.org/TR/rif-overview/
- **Status:** FETCHED_AND_VERIFIED
- **Used in:** Chapter 4 (English edition - real-world enrichment)
- **Document status:** W3C Recommendation (2010-06-22)

## What this source establishes for Ch4
The rule ecosystem RL interoperates with: RIF is the W3C suite for exchanging rules among engines; RIF-Core corresponds to Horn logic without function symbols (Datalog), and RIF defines combined RIF+RDF and RIF+OWL 2 semantics - grounding RL's rule-based, Datalog-compatible design goal.

## Safe simplifications
Citing RIF-Core as Datalog-equivalent and RIF's RDF/OWL combinations is safe.

## Dangerous simplifications / limits
Do not conflate RIF (rule interchange) with OWL 2 RL (a profile); they interoperate but are distinct standards.
