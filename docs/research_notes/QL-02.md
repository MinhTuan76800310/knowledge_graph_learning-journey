# QL-02: The DL-Lite Family and Relations

- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.3487

- **Document status:** JAIR 36 (2009)

## What this source establishes for Ch4
The logical foundation of OWL 2 QL: DL-Lite is the family of tractable DLs whose low-data-complexity query answering (LOGSPACE instance checking and positive-existential query answering via first-order rewriting) makes QL suitable for SQL-style rewriting over huge ABoxes.

## Safe simplifications
Citing DL-Lite as the family OWL 2 QL is based on is safe.

## Dangerous simplifications / limits
Do not conflate DL-Lite with EL; DL-Lite excludes property chains, which is a common source of confusion about chains in profiles.
