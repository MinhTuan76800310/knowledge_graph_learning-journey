## Key Points

- Surveys completeness and correctness refinement of knowledge graphs: link prediction (completeness) vs error detection/repair (correctness).
- Links between completeness-correction are important for the living-system maintenance loop.

## Semantic Contract

- Refinement is a key maintenance operation (detect errors, add missing links).
- The book uses this to support the re-validation and re-assessment sections.
- MUST NOT: claim refinement alone constitutes full lifecycle maintenance; the book's scope is broader (governance, monitoring, audit).
