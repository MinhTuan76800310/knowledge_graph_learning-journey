# LLMKG-01: Unifying Large Language Models and Knowledge Graphs: A Roadmap (Zhu et al., 2023)

- **Primary reference:** Zhu, Y., et al. (2023). Unifying Large Language Models and Knowledge Graphs: A Roadmap. arXiv:2306.08302; IEEE TKDE 36(7), 3190-3207 (2024).
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08302
- **Status:** FETCHED_AND_VERIFIED (paper content read, 2026-08-30)
- **Used in:** Chapter 9
- **Canonical topic:** Three-way synergy of LLMs and KGs: KG-enhanced LLMs, LLM-augmented KGs, and synergized frameworks

## Key Points

- KG-enhanced LLMs: injecting KG knowledge into LLMs — via retrieval-augmentation (KG context), structure-aware pre-training, or prompt structure.
- LLM-augmented KGs: LLMs for KG construction/completion — entity extraction, relation extraction, event extraction, KG completion.
- Synergized frameworks: bidirectional knowledge enrichment and joint reasoning.
- Positions RAG over KG as one concrete route for grounding LLM answers in structured knowledge.

## Semantic Contract

- The roadmap is a taxonomy of LLM+KG research directions, not a set of correctness guarantees.
- LLM-generated KG content is candidate knowledge until governed (Ch6/Ch7).
- MUST NOT: cite the roadmap as proof that any specific technique is universally superior; treat LLM output as accepted knowledge.
