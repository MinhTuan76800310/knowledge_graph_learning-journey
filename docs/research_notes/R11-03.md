## Semantic Contract: Domain and Range

Sections 3.1 and 3.2 define `rdfs:domain` and `rdfs:range` as **entailment rules**, not validation constraints.

### rdfs:domain
If `P rdfs:domain C` and `x P y`, then infer `x rdf:type C`.

### rdfs:range
If `P rdfs:range C` and `x P y`, then infer `y rdf:type C`.

**Critical distinction:** These rules ADD information to the graph. They do NOT reject, constrain, or validate data. A triple with a subject that does not match the declared domain is still valid RDF; the entailment simply infers an additional type assertion.

Validation and constraint checking are the responsibility of SHACL (see SH-01), not RDFS.

## Other Key Entailments

- `rdfs:subClassOf`: If `A rdfs:subClassOf B` and `x rdf:type A`, then infer `x rdf:type B`.
- `rdfs:subPropertyOf`: If `P rdfs:subPropertyOf Q` and `x P y`, then infer `x Q y`.
