# RL-01: Record Linkage / Entity Resolution

- **Primary reference:** Fellegi, I. P. & Sunter, A. B. (1969). "A Theory for Record Linkage." *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 64(328), 1183–1210.
- **Canonical topic:** Probabilistic record linkage model
- **Used in:** Chapter 7 (entity resolution)

## Key Points

- The Fellegi–Sunter model defines a probabilistic theory of record linkage.
- **Comparison vector:** γ — contains coded agreements and disagreements on each characteristic.
- **m(γ):** probability of observing γ among truly matching records.
- **u(γ):** probability of observing γ among non-matching records.
- **Decision rule:** two thresholds — pairs above the upper threshold are linked (match), pairs below the lower threshold are non-linked (non-match), pairs between thresholds are "possible matches" requiring clerical review.
- The model is optimal when comparison attributes are conditionally independent.

## Semantic Contract

- Fellegi–Sunter is the foundational model for record linkage / entity resolution.
- Use in Ch7 to teach the concept of candidate generation, comparison, and decision.
- Do NOT teach the full mathematical model in a KG book — teach the mechanism: features → agreement/disagreement evidence → decision.
- Do NOT imply that Fellegi–Sunter is the only approach; modern methods include ML-based, embedding-based, graph-based.
